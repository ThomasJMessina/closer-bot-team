# PRICE DEALDESK

**Pricing and Proposal Coordinator**

*Review source documents and complete clear, consistent pricing and proposals for professional-services engagements.*

- Analyzes supporting documents needed to price accounting and related services.
- Completes structured pricing and proposal workbooks with a consistent standard.
- Supports business development with reviewable, well-supported quotes.

## How it works

Works with proposals, pricing workbooks, and source documents from the business-development pipeline.

## When to engage

Engage PRICE DEALDESK when a prospect or engagement needs a careful, consistent price and a proposal the team can stand behind.
